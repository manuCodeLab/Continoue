Our Little Story 📖

Happy Birthday to the girl who became the most unexpected and beautiful part of my life ❤️

If someone had told me on my birthday that the girl who wished me that day would someday become this important to me, I honestly wouldn’t have believed them.

I had seen you in college before, but you were just someone I knew by face. I never thought much about it. I definitely never imagined that one simple birthday wish from you would become the beginning of our story.

But that day was different. You wished me, and somehow we started talking. I don't even know what happened to me that day, but I remember talking to you so much. 😂 I didn't expect myself to get so comfortable with you or to keep wanting to talk to you.

At that time, I had no idea where all of this was going. I wasn't thinking about love or relationships. I was just enjoying talking to you and getting to know you.

Then, slowly... day by day, our conversations became more important to me. I started looking forward to talking to you. I started caring about you. I started noticing the little things about you.

And somewhere between all those conversations, laughs, random talks, and the time we spent getting to know each other, something changed inside me.

I don't know exactly when it happened, but I realized that you weren't just another person in my life anymore.

You became my person. ❤️

The girl I never expected to get this close to became the girl I don't want to lose.

Sometimes I still think about how everything started—with just a birthday wish. It's crazy how one small moment can completely change your life.

And today, I just want you to know that I'm genuinely so grateful for that moment, for you, and for everything that came after it.

You are one of the most precious people in my life, and I really, really love you, undama. ❤️🥹

Happy Birthday, my love. 🎂❤️

I hope this is just the beginning of many more birthdays, memories, fights, laughs, stupid conversations, and beautiful moments together.

Our story may have started with a birthday wish, but I hope it never ends. ❤️
